# VaaniOS — AI-Powered Voice-Based Customer Support Platform

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional customer support often involves long waiting times, repetitive manual complaint entry, and difficulty tracking customer issues. Support teams need a more efficient way to understand, prioritize, and manage customer complaints.

## 2. Proposed Solution
VaaniOS is an AI-powered voice-based customer support platform that transforms spoken customer complaints into structured support tickets. It combines speech recognition, AI-based complaint analysis, automated voice responses, and human support escalation.

## 3. How VaaniOS Works
1. **Voice Input:** Customers explain their complaints using voice.
2. **Speech-to-Text:** Gnani AI Prisma V3 converts speech into text.
3. **AI Analysis:** Gemini analyzes the complaint, identifies its category and priority, and generates an empathetic customer response.
4. **Text-to-Speech:** Gnani AI Timbre V2.5 converts responses into speech.
5. **Ticket Creation:** The system creates structured support tickets for administrators.
6. **Human Escalation:** Complex complaints can be escalated to a support agent.
7. **Live Chat:** Customers can communicate with their assigned support agents.

## 4. Key Features
- Voice-based complaint submission.
- AI-powered complaint understanding and prioritization.
- Automatic support ticket creation.
- Separate customer and administrator dashboards.
- Human escalation and ticket assignment.
- Real-time customer-agent communication.
- Speech-to-text and text-to-speech integration.

## 5. Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB, Mongoose
- **AI:** Gemini API
- **Speech AI:** Gnani AI Prisma V3 (STT), Timbre V2.5 (TTS)
- **Real-Time Communication:** Socket.IO
- **Authentication:** JWT

## 6. Expected Impact
VaaniOS aims to reduce manual effort in customer support, improve complaint organization, and make support more accessible through voice interaction. It combines AI automation with human assistance so customers can receive structured and efficient support.
